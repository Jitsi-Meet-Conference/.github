# Jitsi Meet - Conference, Video Call, and Room Link

## About Jitsi Meet

Jitsi Meet is a meeting client. Jitsi Meet Conference is the named meeting. Jitsi Meet Video Call is the live session in that room. Jitsi Meet Room Link is the URL you share with people who belong there. Jitsi Meet Screen Share is a window or display you choose. Jitsi Meet Self Host is your own server when policy wants that.

Open or join a Jitsi Meet Conference you operate or were invited to. Start Jitsi Meet Video Call on a server you trust or were given. Share Jitsi Meet Room Link only with the roster. Use Jitsi Meet Screen Share of a window, not the whole display, when only one window matters. Use Jitsi Meet Self Host when the architecture says the meeting stays on your host.

Jitsi Meet Conference, Jitsi Meet Video Call, Jitsi Meet Room Link, Jitsi Meet Screen Share, and Jitsi Meet Self Host exist to talk and show, on infrastructure you may use.

## Overview

![Jitsi Meet](https://fedoramagazine.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/12/jitsi_interface.png)

Jitsi Meet is a room plus media. Jitsi Meet Conference is the name. Jitsi Meet Video Call is the session. Jitsi Meet Room Link is how people arrive. Jitsi Meet Screen Share is the picture you push. Jitsi Meet Self Host is where the room lives when you run the server.

Do not paste a live Jitsi Meet Room Link on a public page if the meeting is not public. Do not leave Jitsi Meet Conference open after the agenda. Jitsi Meet Screen Share should be a window when a window is enough. Jitsi Meet Self Host still needs a moderator. Jitsi Meet Video Call is not a kiosk unless that is the job.

## Key Features of Jitsi Meet

- **Conference as the named meeting** – Jitsi Meet Conference is the room title and roster intent. Jitsi Meet Video Call runs inside it. Jitsi Meet Room Link is how invitees arrive. Jitsi Meet Screen Share is optional. Jitsi Meet Self Host is optional unless policy requires it.
- **Video call as the session** – Jitsi Meet Video Call is camera, mic, and peers. Mute by default. Jitsi Meet Conference still has a name. Jitsi Meet Room Link should not be a hallway. Jitsi Meet Screen Share adds encoder load. Jitsi Meet Self Host does not skip a moderator.
- **Room link as the door** – Jitsi Meet Room Link is a URL you send to people who belong. Time-box it. Jitsi Meet Conference can still use a lobby. Jitsi Meet Video Call starts after join. Jitsi Meet Screen Share stays off until asked. Jitsi Meet Self Host changes the host in that URL.
- **Screen share as a chosen window** – Jitsi Meet Screen Share is one window or a display you pick. Prefer a window. Jitsi Meet Video Call continues. Jitsi Meet Conference roster should still be people who may see that window. Jitsi Meet Room Link does not grant the share by itself. Jitsi Meet Self Host does not change that.
- **Self host as your server** – Jitsi Meet Self Host is when you run the meeting server. Use it when policy wants traffic on your machines. Jitsi Meet Conference still needs a name. Jitsi Meet Room Link then points at you. Jitsi Meet Video Call still needs a roster. Jitsi Meet Screen Share still needs a reason.

## Quick Start with Jitsi Meet

1. **One room** – Create a Jitsi Meet Conference. Copy Jitsi Meet Room Link to people who belong. Join Jitsi Meet Video Call. Mute. Ignore Jitsi Meet Screen Share. Ignore Jitsi Meet Self Host if you were given a public host you may use.
2. **Share a window** – During Jitsi Meet Video Call, start Jitsi Meet Screen Share of one window. Confirm the roster in Jitsi Meet Conference. Jitsi Meet Room Link unchanged. Stop share when done. Jitsi Meet Self Host unchanged.
3. **Self host only if policy** – Point Jitsi Meet Self Host at a server you operate. Create Jitsi Meet Conference there. Send that Jitsi Meet Room Link. Test Jitsi Meet Video Call. Test Jitsi Meet Screen Share. Close when the agenda ends.
4. **Close the door** – End Jitsi Meet Video Call. Destroy or lock Jitsi Meet Conference. Do not leave Jitsi Meet Room Link on a wiki. Stop Jitsi Meet Screen Share. Jitsi Meet Self Host still needs its own patch cycle.
5. **Review** – Jitsi Meet Conference names still match the job. Jitsi Meet Room Link not public unless the meeting is. Jitsi Meet Video Call not idle. Jitsi Meet Screen Share not a kiosk. Jitsi Meet Self Host still in scope.

## Who Will Like Jitsi Meet

- **Operators** – Named Jitsi Meet Conference, Jitsi Meet Room Link to the rotation, Jitsi Meet Video Call for a bridge, rare Jitsi Meet Screen Share, Jitsi Meet Self Host if the platform already has one.
- **Admins** – Jitsi Meet Self Host they operate, lobby on Jitsi Meet Conference, Jitsi Meet Room Link policy, Jitsi Meet Video Call, Jitsi Meet Screen Share rules.
- **Developers** – Standup Jitsi Meet Conference, Jitsi Meet Video Call, Jitsi Meet Screen Share of a window, Jitsi Meet Room Link in the calendar, skip Jitsi Meet Self Host if a shared host exists.
- **Support** – Customer Jitsi Meet Conference, Jitsi Meet Room Link they were sent, Jitsi Meet Video Call, Jitsi Meet Screen Share of a named window, no Jitsi Meet Self Host unless the desk runs one.
- **Anyone running meetings they own** – Jitsi Meet Conference, Jitsi Meet Video Call, Jitsi Meet Room Link, optional Jitsi Meet Screen Share, optional Jitsi Meet Self Host.
